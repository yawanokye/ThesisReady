# Research Journey Complete Guide Upgrade — 2026-08-16

ProjectReady AI is now organised as a guided research-development environment rather than a chapter generator with separate add-ons.

## Five visible top-level modules

1. **Research Journey** — the flagship end-to-end workspace.
2. **Quick Chapter Development** — direct access to the existing full chapter/selected-section drafting workflow.
3. **Review & Strengthen** — the existing Chapter Strengthener expanded into six review modes.
4. **Research Coach** — Explain, Guide me, Help me decide, and Viva preparation modes.
5. **My Research Projects** — recovery, progress and continue-next-step access.

Topic Ideas remains available as an early-stage supporting tool rather than one of the five main research-workspace modules.

## Research Journey stages

The Research Journey now presents twelve stages:

1. Research Setup
2. Problem & Research Logic
3. Evidence & Literature
4. Theory & Conceptual Framework
5. Research Design
6. Develop Chapters
7. Review & Strengthen
8. Data & Analysis
9. Supervisor Corrections
10. Final Research Audit
11. Compile Thesis
12. Viva Preparation

The path is adaptive for quantitative, qualitative and mixed-methods projects. Qualitative work emphasises philosophical position, participants/cases, coding and trustworthiness. Mixed-methods work adds design and integration requirements. Quantitative work retains constructs, hypotheses where appropriate, sampling, measurement and objective-linked analysis.

## My Research Record

A persistent Research Record stores the authoritative project facts used across chapters and review workflows. It includes theory, conceptual framework, design, philosophy, population/participants, sample basis, sampling, instruments, validity/reliability, trustworthiness, ethics, analysis plan, coding approach and mixed-methods integration.

## Researcher-owned decision checkpoints

Major theory, design, sampling, analysis and conceptual-framework choices are stored as explicit research decisions. ProjectReady may explain and recommend, but the researcher confirms the selection and rationale.

## What do I need next?

The research logic engine now returns an adaptive `what_next` action and the exact missing information for the next research stage. The main Research Journey dashboard displays this recommendation and links directly to the relevant task.

## Review & Strengthen modes

The existing strengthener now includes:

- Strengthen Chapter
- Resolve Citation Gaps
- Apply Supervisor Corrections
- Check Research Alignment
- Check Results & Interpretation
- Review Methodology

The existing evidence-preserving, claim-support, citation-density, selected-paper and tracked DOCX workflows remain intact.

## Supervisor corrections

Supervisor comments can be stored as trackable project actions with chapter/location, status and resolution notes. Statuses are Open, Addressing, Resolved and Ignored. Ignoring a correction requires a recorded reason.

## Final Research Audit

The final workflow audit checks chapter coverage, objective-question alignment, research-decision confirmation, evidence base, stored Claim Support Review status, tracked supervisor corrections, cross-chapter logic and academic compliance checks. It is explicitly a workflow audit and does not replace supervisor, examiner or institutional approval.

## Research Coach

The Research Coach uses the saved project record and supports:

- Explain
- Guide me
- Help me decide
- Viva preparation

It is instructed not to invent facts, sources, statistics, data, methods, approvals or theories. If the OpenAI API is unavailable, a deterministic project-aware guidance fallback remains available.

## Backward compatibility

The existing `/workspace` and `/chapter-strengthener` routes remain supported. New routes include `/research-journey`, `/quick-chapter`, `/review-strengthen`, `/research-coach`, and `/my-projects`.
