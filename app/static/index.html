How it works

From research idea to a coherent, supervisor-ready working thesis

01

Set up research logic

Confirm the topic, problem direction, objectives, questions, hypotheses or qualitative focus, constructs and study context.

02

Build the Research Record

Record theory, conceptual framework, design, sampling, instruments, ethics and analysis decisions as the project's single source of truth.

03

Develop with verified evidence

Find literature, upload selected papers, develop or strengthen chapters, and resolve unsupported claims without fabricated citations.

04

Review, correct and defend

Track supervisor corrections, check results and interpretation, run the final research audit, compile the working thesis and prepare for viva.

Core features

Built for real academic project work

The platform adapts to the student’s level, chapter format, research approach and evidence supplied.

Adaptive Research Journey

Shows what is complete, what is missing and what the researcher should do next for quantitative, qualitative or mixed-methods work.

My Research Record

Keeps authoritative research decisions and facts consistent across chapter development, strengthening, analysis and final review.

Guided Chapter Development

Develops editable working drafts for Chapters One to Five from user-selected sections, supplied research materials and school-specific requirements.

Research Source Finder

Searches open scholarly metadata sources and attaches relevant papers before working-draft development.

Literature Review Assistant

Supports conceptual, theoretical, empirical, objective-based, thematic and econometric reviews.

Methodology Builder

Adapts to primary data, secondary data, qualitative, mixed methods and econometric studies.

Results Upload

Uses uploaded Excel, CSV, Word, PDF or text results to support Chapter Four working-draft development.

Compliance Review

Checks drafts against selected guideline requirements and exports a DOCX checklist report.

Review & Strengthen

Strengthens existing chapters, resolves citation gaps, applies supervisor comments, reviews methodology, checks research alignment and tests results interpretation.

Research Coach

Explains concepts, guides decisions, compares alternatives and supports viva preparation without silently making researcher-owned decisions.

Academic integrity and responsible use

Software-guided research development, not ghostwriting

Users must provide and control the research direction, materials and academic decisions. ProjectReady AI produces editable working drafts and review guidance that require verification, revision and appropriate supervisor or institutional approval.
