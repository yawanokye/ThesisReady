ProjectReady selects the matrix from the discipline/programme above. Values are individual verified or user-supplied referenced works per 1,000 substantive words. The range guides evidence density and is never filled with invented or padded citations.

Fail-closed rule: if suitable verified evidence cannot be found, ProjectReady leaves the section below target and flags the evidence gap. It never fabricates an author, year, DOI, source, statistic or finding.
