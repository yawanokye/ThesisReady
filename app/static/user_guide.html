Single source of truth

My Research Record

The Research Record stores the approved facts and decisions that later chapters and analysis must respect.

  • Approved title, objectives, questions and hypotheses
  • Theory, constructs and variable roles
  • Research design, population, sample and instruments
  • Analysis plan and confirmed results
  • Verified statistics, selected sources and supervisor decisions

Evidence before assertion

Literature, selected papers and citation verification

ProjectReady combines automatically discovered scholarly records with literature deliberately supplied by the researcher.

My Selected Papers

Upload up to 50 selected papers. Relevant papers can support chapter development, literature review, theory, methodology, discussion and other evidence-led sections.

Find Literature

Search supported scholarly metadata and evidence services, deduplicate results and attach the strongest relevant records to the project.

Verified Citation Density

Use the discipline and section matrix together with a paragraph target of at least 2 verified sources and preferably 3 where suitable evidence genuinely exists.

Before final output

Claim Support Review

ProjectReady identifies evidence-bearing claims that remain unsupported. The student decides what happens to each item.

1Claim flaggedUnsupported evidence-bearing claim is highlighted.
2Find verified sourcesThe button shows Searching... while supported databases are checked.
3Inspect evidenceReview title, author, year, DOI or URL and accessible support text.
4Approve or ignoreApprove genuine support or Ignore when the claim does not require a source.
5Apply citationsOnly approved verified citations are inserted. Obsolete source-needed placeholders are removed.
6Final approvalSee citations added, unique sources used, items ignored and unresolved gaps.
No source, no citation.ProjectReady must not invent authors, years, titles, DOIs, URLs, statistics or findings to satisfy citation-density targets.

Calculation-first analysis

Data & Analysis Workspace

Upload real data and let statistical libraries compute the results. AI may explain verified output but must not manufacture coefficients, p-values, confidence intervals, model-fit indices or findings.

1

Data Setup

Upload CSV/XLSX, identify variables and inspect data structure.

2

Quality & Descriptives

Missing data, duplicates, distributions, frequencies, descriptive statistics and reliability where applicable.

3

Analysis Selection

ProjectReady recommends options from objectives, variable roles and data structure. Researcher confirmation is required.

4

Diagnostics & Assumptions

Each method carries its own assumptions and diagnostic checks.

5

Model Estimation

Run the confirmed statistical, econometric, SEM, network, qualitative or mixed-method workflow.

6

Validate & Report

Generate exact tables, consistency checks, professional diagrams and Chapter Four-ready interpretation.

Comparisons

t-tests, ANOVA, Welch ANOVA, factorial ANOVA, ANCOVA and MANOVA with appropriate assumptions and post-hoc procedures.

Regression & conditional process

CLRM/OLS, robust and hierarchical regression, quantile regression, GLMs, mediation, moderation, moderated mediation and multilevel models.

Panel & SEM

Pooled, fixed-effects and random-effects panel regression plus SEM/path analysis. SEM provides a professional model diagram from fitted paths.

Time series & econometrics

AR, MA, ARMA, ARIMA, SARIMAX, DOLS, ARDL/NARDL, VAR/SVAR, TVP-VAR, Engle-Granger, Johansen, VECM and decomposition workflows.

Volatility, network & signal

ARCH/GARCH variants, DCC-GARCH, network analysis with professional diagrams, DWT/MODWT and EMD-family methods.

Forecasting & advanced ML

Tree ensembles, Prophet-family models and capability-gated deep or foundation forecasting models. ProjectReady never substitutes a simpler model and labels it as a requested specialist model.

Conceptual framework is optional.If supplied, it assists variable-role and path mapping. It is not required to run an analysis.

Before academic submission

Final Research Audit

ProjectReady can identify readiness problems, but academic approval remains with the researcher, supervisor and institution.

AlignmentTitle, problem, objectives, questions, hypotheses, methods, results and conclusions agree.
EvidenceUnsupported claims are resolved and references match verified sources.
StatisticsTables, coefficients, p-values, confidence intervals and prose are consistent.
Research decisionsRequired researcher checkpoints have been confirmed.
Supervisor correctionsOpen comments are resolved or explicitly retained with a reason.
Human reviewThe student and supervisor review the working document before submission.

Responsible use

ProjectReady supports research development. It must not fabricate data, findings, ethics approval, citations or source content. Treat generated material as editable working material that requires researcher judgement, source checking and supervisor review.
