Quick Chapter Development

Develop a chapter within your Research Journey

Only the main research information is shown by default. Optional evidence, style, recovery and alignment tools stay collapsed until you need them.

1. Set up your research project

Complete the essential fields first. Open the optional groups only when you have more evidence, style guidance, recovery details or school-specific instructions.

Core profile and routing

Chapter depth and custom page target

Use the default level-based target, or set a custom page range for the active chapter. Very long chapters, especially PhD literature reviews, are developed in staged section batches instead of one shallow pass.

Default target will be used unless you set a custom range.

Format, evidence and study context
School-specific format notes

The app completes what it can from the project profile and relevant sources. It leaves a red action only for material information that cannot be inferred or retrieved safely.

Citation-density standard

ProjectReady uses verified referenced works per 1,000 substantive words. The range guides evidence density and is never a licence to invent or pad citations.

DisciplineIntroductionLiterature ReviewMethodologyResults & DiscussionConclusion

Only verified or user-supplied sources count. If the evidence bank is insufficient, the draft stays below target and flags the evidence gap instead of fabricating a citation.

Evidence notes and Chapter One autofill

Autofill from Introduction or Chapter One

Upload an existing Introduction or Chapter One to extract likely title, research area, context, objectives, questions and variables. Review every field after autofill.

Find literature sources for this study

        
Search open scholarly metadata sources and attach only highly or partly relevant papers before drafting. Use my selected papers

          
Optional. Upload up to 50 papers that you want ProjectReady to consider alongside the literature it finds automatically. All uploaded papers are represented in a compact evidence map for synthesis; only the most relevant original passages are sent to the writing model for the active section. This keeps all 50 available to enrich the work without repeatedly transmitting all 50 papers. ProjectReady still checks claim support and will never cite a paper merely because it was uploaded.

          
          

            
          

          
PDF and DOCX papers are text-extracted. If a DOI can be verified, the paper becomes citation-ready automatically. Otherwise, the paper can support reading and synthesis, but you must confirm its author, year and title before ProjectReady creates a new citation from it.

Academic integrity and user responsibility

        
ProjectReady AI is a self-service research-development tool. It does not employ writers, accept ghostwriting engagements, sell completed academic work, fabricate data or references, or submit work to an institution on a user's behalf.

        
        
        
ProjectReady AI produces editable working drafts and research guidance, not completed or submission-ready academic work. Read the Academic Integrity and Responsible Use Policy.

Claim Support Review

          Review required
        

        
ProjectReady highlights evidence-bearing claims that still have no citation and paragraphs below the 2-3 verified-source target. ProjectReady first tries to meet the discipline-specific citation-density target during drafting. Only unresolved evidence gaps are listed here. Search OpenAlex, Crossref, Semantic Scholar, ERIC, DataCite, Europe PMC and PubMed, inspect the evidence, then approve only sources that genuinely support the statement. Google Scholar is available as a manual external search because it is not scraped. This review is strongly recommended but does not block Word export, so you can use the exported working draft to guide reading and source checking.
